# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-04)

First release.

- **Live timing:**
  - status banner and event pop-ups
  - track position line, with a gap view
  - timing tower with mini-sectors, tyres and positions gained or lost
  - driver focus panel
  - race control messages
- **Dashboard and season data:** dashboard, driver and constructor standings, season calendar,
  and race / qualifying / sprint results for any season.
- **Next race:** weekend sessions with a live countdown.
- **Offline cache:** for standings and results data.
